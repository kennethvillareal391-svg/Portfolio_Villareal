St. Mary's College of Meycauayan (2025 - present)

St. Mary's College of Meycauayan was founded in 1916 by a local parish priest as the Escuela Parroquial de Meycauayan, a small parochial primary school focused heavily on Catholic catechism. Entrusted to the Religious of the Virgin Mary (RVM)—the first Filipino congregation—the school progressively expanded its academic offerings, officially renaming itself St. Mary's Academy when it reopened in 1945 following a brief operational pause during World War II. Despite a devastating fire that completely razed its original campus on April 4, 1949, the institution persevered through temporary residential classes and eventually relocated to a larger, modern campus along MacArthur Highway in Barangay Saluysoy, formally transitioning into a higher education institution under its current name in 1986

Colegio de Sto. Niño (2024 - 2025)

Colegio de Sto. Niño, Meycauayan Campus was established as part of a modern, multi-branch private educational network spanning Bulacan and Caloocan to provide accessible, career-oriented training to local youth. Located within the EMA Town Center in Barangay Camalig, Meycauayan City, the institution gained official momentum between 2016 and 2017 when it secured Department of Education (DepEd) permits to launch its Senior High School (SHS) technical-vocational and academic tracks. Over the years, the campus fortified its status in the region by becoming an accredited Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) assessment center, later expanding its curriculum amidst changing educational landscapes to launch a comprehensive Junior High School department in 2023.

West City Central School (2015 - 2018)

West City Central School, located along Ilaya Road in Barangay Carmen, Cagayan de Oro City, was established during the mid-20th century as a vital public educational institution to serve the rapidly growing population on the western side of the Cagayan river. Over the decades, it has grown to become the second-largest central elementary school in the city, transforming generations of local youth into functionally literate and socially responsible citizens. While the institution has faced operational hurdles over the years—including localized fires that damaged older campus sections—it has continually bounced back through community resilience and massive local government investments. Today, the school boasts modern multi-story classroom buildings equipped with advanced multimedia packages, eliminating classroom shortages and cementing its status as a premier hub for primary and special science elementary education in Northern Mindanao.
